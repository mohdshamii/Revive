About Revive AI

Clinical Diagnostics Engineered for Precision

Revive is an enterprise-grade artificial intelligence platform developed to empower clinical practitioners, oncologists, cardiologists, and researchers with rapid, transparent, and verified disease risk classification.

Our Mission

Bridging Computational Intelligence and Patient Care

Diagnostic delays and false negatives remain critical challenges in modern healthcare. Revive eliminates barriers to predictive analytics by operationalizing tested machine learning ensembles (Random Forests) and deep convolutional neural networks directly in an intuitive, accessible web interface.

Every diagnostic module is grounded strictly in genuine clinical benchmark datasets, with complete visibility into algorithm hyperparameters, holdout test metrics, confusion matrices, and ROC-AUC scores.

Core Architecture Principles
  • Zero Mock Telemetry: Real holdout evaluation metrics computed on genuine clinical cases.
  • Sanitized Pipelines: Automated missing-value median imputation and categorical mapping.
  • Privacy First: Local server execution with zero third-party telemetry sharing.
  • Jupyter Integration: Retrain and evaluate models directly inside interactive notebook cells.
Verified Datasets

Pre-packaged with 8 peer-reviewed clinical datasets totaling over 40,000 real patient observations and microscopic scans.

Random Forest Ensembles

High-capacity 100-estimator decision forests delivering robust variance reduction and non-linear feature interaction capture.

Convolutional Vision

Multi-stage Conv2D and Batch Normalization architectures for cellular blood smear and radiograph pattern extraction.

RESTful Microservices

Fully decoupled JSON endpoints enabling seamless EHR and clinical decision support system (CDSS) integrations.
