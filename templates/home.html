Clinical Disease Predictors

Diabetes

Diabetes occurs when blood glucose is elevated. Analyzes glucose levels, insulin, blood pressure, BMI, and genetic pedigree.

Symptoms
  • Frequent urination & intense thirst
  • Persistent fatigue and blurry vision
Launch Diabetes Predictor →

Breast Cancer

Malignancy assessment based on fine-needle aspirate cell nuclei radius, texture, concavity, and perimeter dimensions.

Symptoms
  • Palpable breast lump or tissue thickening
  • Skin alterations, redness, dimpling
Launch Cancer Predictor →

Heart Disease

Coronary artery disease evaluation analyzing resting blood pressure, cholesterol, resting ECG, ST depression, and angina.

Symptoms
  • Chest discomfort, pain, breathlessness
  • Cardiac palpitations and unexplained fatigue
Launch Heart Predictor →

Chronic Kidney Disease

Renal function analysis assessing albumin, blood urea, serum creatinine, hypertension, and cellular urine markers.

Symptoms
  • Nausea, vomiting, lethargy
  • Fluid retention, muscle cramps, hypertension
Launch Kidney Predictor →

Liver Disease

Hepatic damage screening using total and direct bilirubin, alkaline phosphatase, ALT/AST transaminases, and albumin ratios.

Symptoms
  • Jaundice (yellowing of eyes/skin)
  • Abdominal tenderness and swelling
Launch Liver Predictor →

Brain Stroke

Cerebrovascular risk assessment based on hypertension, heart conditions, blood glucose, BMI, and lifestyle factors.

Symptoms
  • Sudden numbness or facial drooping
  • Loss of speech clarity or motor balance
Launch Stroke Predictor →

Malaria (Deep Learning CNN)

Microscopic blood smear image analysis classifying erythrocytes into uninfected or parasitized malarial cells.

Symptoms
  • Cyclical high fevers, shaking chills
  • Severe headache, nausea, sweating
Launch Malaria CNN Classifier →

Pneumonia (Deep Learning CNN)

Pediatric chest X-Ray (radiograph) neural classifier diagnosing pulmonary consolidation and inflammatory opacity.

Symptoms
  • Cough producing purulent phlegm
  • High fever, tachypnea, shortness of breath
