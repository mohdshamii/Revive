Frequently Asked Questions

How are patient biomarker predictions calculated?

Patient physiological values are normalized using standard scaler artifacts and processed through trained Random Forest ensembles. For image modalities (malaria thin smears and pneumonia radiographs), normalized pixel arrays are evaluated by convolutional neural networks.

Does Revive transmit sensitive health data to external cloud APIs?

No. Revive runs 100% locally on your server. All dataset processing, feature extraction, and neural inferences happen directly within your Python/Flask execution environment with zero third-party telemetry sharing.

Can clinical practitioners retrain models with hospital-specific datasets?

Yes. The interactive ML Pipeline module (accessible via the top navigation bar at /training) allows practitioners to adjust split ratios and tree estimators, triggering live retraining and updated metric persistence.
